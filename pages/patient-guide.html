PATIENT GUIDE

Before · During · After

A little less wondering.

Your first visit doesn't need much preparation. Here's what to expect, what to bring and a few simple things that can make your appointment easier.

AT A GLANCE

Your visit, step by step.

01

Before you arrive

Complete any information requested during booking and bring relevant dental records if you have them.

02

When you arrive

Check in with our reception team. If it is your first visit, arriving around 10 minutes early is helpful.

03

During your consultation

Your dentist will talk with you about your concerns, examine your teeth and explain the next steps.

04

Before you leave

You will have time to ask questions and understand any recommended treatment or follow-up.

YOUR FIRST VISIT 45–60 minutes, depending on your appointment

YOUR FIRST VISIT

Come as you are.

There is no need to prepare a list of dental terms or know exactly what treatment you need. Tell us what has been bothering you and we'll take the conversation from there.

It helps to bring:

  • ✓ Previous dental records or X-rays
  • ✓ A list of current medications
  • ✓ Any questions you've been meaning to ask
  • ✓ Your appointment details

BEFORE YOUR VISIT

A few simple preparations.

Most appointments don't require special preparation. These small things can make the visit smoother.

01

Eat normally

Unless your dentist has given you different instructions, you can follow your normal routine.

02

Arrive a little early

Ten minutes gives you enough time to settle in and complete any remaining details.

03

Bring your questions

Write them down if that helps. There is no such thing as a silly question at HASYA.

04

Tell us if you're nervous

Let the team know before your visit. We can take a little more time to explain what will happen.

IN THE DENTAL CHAIR

Nothing should feel unexplained.

01

Conversation

We'll start by understanding why you're here and what you'd like help with.

02

Examination

Your dentist will examine your teeth and gums and discuss anything that needs attention.

03

Explanation

We'll explain what we find and talk through suitable next steps.

04

Your choice

You'll have space to ask questions and understand your options before deciding what comes next.

AFTER YOUR VISIT

Keep the conversation going.

Every treatment has different aftercare instructions. Your dentist will explain anything specific to your appointment.

01

Follow your instructions

Take note of any medication, cleaning or food instructions given to you.

02

Give yourself time

Some treatments may leave your mouth feeling different for a short period. Follow the advice provided by your dentist.

03

Ask if you're unsure

If something doesn't feel right or you are unsure about your instructions, contact the clinic.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Still wondering about something?

How early should I arrive?

Around 10 minutes before your scheduled appointment is usually enough, especially for a first visit.

Can I bring someone with me?

Yes. If having someone with you makes your visit more comfortable, let our reception team know when you arrive.

What if I need to change my appointment?

Call the clinic as soon as possible. We'll help you find another suitable time.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call the clinic and describe the situation to our team. We will advise you on the next available option.

Can I ask about treatment costs?

Yes. Your dentist can explain the recommended treatment and associated costs after assessing your individual needs.
