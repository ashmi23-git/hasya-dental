TREATMENT QUESTIONS

Still wondering what you need?

That is exactly what your consultation is for.

How do I know which treatment I need?

You do not have to decide before your visit. We begin with your concerns and an examination, then explain the relevant options.

Can I ask about more than one treatment?

Yes. If you have several concerns, mention them during your appointment so they can be considered together.

Will I know the treatment plan beforehand?

We explain the recommended next steps before proceeding with planned treatment.

Can I book a consultation first?

Yes. If you are unsure what kind of care you need, you can simply request a consultation.
