OUR APPROACH

The dentist's job starts before the treatment.

01

We ask questions.

Before suggesting treatment, we want to understand what brought you in and what you would like to improve.

02

We explain your options.

You should know what is being suggested, why it may help and what alternatives are available.

03

We give you time.

Not every decision needs to happen immediately. We are happy to answer questions before you decide.

04

We keep things clear.

From treatment plans to aftercare, we keep the information practical and easy to understand.
