BEFORE YOUR VISIT

A few things you may wonder.

What if I don't know which treatment I need?

That's completely fine. Choose "Something else" in the form and describe what you're experiencing. Your dentist can discuss the appropriate next steps during your visit.

Can I request a specific dentist?

Yes. You can select a preferred dentist when making your request. We'll do our best to match your preferred time and dentist.

What if I need to change my appointment?

Please contact the clinic as soon as possible if you need to reschedule. We'll help you find another suitable time.

Is the appointment confirmed immediately?

No. The form sends an appointment request. Our team will contact you to confirm the date and time before the appointment is final.
