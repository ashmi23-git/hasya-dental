COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you visit us.

A few answers to questions patients often ask before their appointment.

Do I need an appointment?

We recommend booking an appointment so we can give you enough time with the dentist.

What should I bring?

Bring any previous dental records or reports you have, along with a list of medicines if relevant.

Can I ask questions during my appointment?

Absolutely. We encourage questions and will explain your treatment options before proceeding.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We provide age-appropriate dental care for children and teenagers.
