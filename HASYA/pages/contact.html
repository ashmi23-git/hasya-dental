BEFORE YOU VISIT

A few quick answers.

Do I need an appointment?

We recommend booking ahead so we can give you enough time with the dentist. If you have an urgent concern, call us and we'll let you know what options are available.

Can I ask about treatment prices?

Yes. Treatment costs depend on your individual needs. Our team can explain the expected options and costs after your consultation.

What should I bring?

Bring any previous dental records, scans or prescriptions that may be useful. If this is your first visit, your basic identification details are enough to get started.

What if I am nervous about dentists?

Let our team know when you book. We'll take things at a comfortable pace and explain what we're doing before we begin.
